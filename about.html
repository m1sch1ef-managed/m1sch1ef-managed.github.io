Collect first.

M1sch1ef Managed is a collector-led corner of the hobby: free Pokémon card games, condition debates, a card shop simulator, and the questionable decisions that make collecting fun.

Guess slab grades in Grade This Card, inspect listing photos in Condition Detective, or build your own shop in Card Vendor Sim. All 14 arcade games are free to play in your browser without an account.

Prefer action? Play Redline Rogue motorcycle racing, build a delivery business in Paper Route Empire, or chase a high score in Classic Snake. Between games, explore our daily TCG news and market snapshots.

We’re independent and not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Pokémon, Nintendo, Creatures, or any grading company. Card challenges use photos from our eBay listings; Card Vendor Sim uses simulated artwork and prices.
