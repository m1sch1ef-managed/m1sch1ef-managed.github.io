Read the text transcript

Scene summaries and dialogue, in reading order. Contains this edition’s story.

Cover

The Beast Beneath Bell Row, Edition 4 of The Hollow Hoard: cover.

Page 1 of 12

A deep knocking shakes the occupied Bell Row quay. Ren and Tamsin arrive as water starts breaking through its stone seams.

SFX: KONK... KONK... KONK

PARENT: Away from the edge.

REN: This is where they sent number three.

TAMSIN: Then we're late.

Page 2 of 12

Workers connect a transport tank marked 003 to the pump. Inside, the captive Bellray taps against the glass under Dorn’s supervision.

WORKER: Keep the ray level.

REN: It's calling.

REN: Foreman Dorn?

DORN: It finds obstructions. New pump does the pushing.

TAMSIN: How long has it been working?

DORN: Since midday.

Page 3 of 12

Ren shows Dorn the transfer evidence while Tamsin points out the leaking quay. The crew checks the gauges as the pump starts.

REN: The same system nearly killed another creature.

DORN: This is a delivery record. I need a fault I can test.

TAMSIN: Then watch the return pressure.

DORN: Call every rise.

Page 4 of 12

Pressure rises too far. Ren follows Bellray’s repeated knocks toward a blocked side pipe and a covered gate.

WORKER: Return rising.

REN: Not random. It's pointing.

REN: It gets sharp here.

TAMSIN: An old guild return.

Page 5 of 12

Tamsin identifies the old bypass. A museum order required it sealed, contradicting the dangerous pressure on the gauge.

TAMSIN: That return should open before the stroke.

DORN: We were told the old line wasn't safe.

REN: Sealing it traps the discharge.

SFX: KONK

Page 6 of 12

The museum agent demands the test continue. Dorn pauses the pump and Tamsin stops the agent reaching its live control as the walkway cracks.

AGENT: The Curator promised a working pump.

DORN: Then we should make sure it works.

TAMSIN: Touch that and you own the flood.

SFX: KRRK

Page 7 of 12

Ren plans an evacuation route, Tamsin checks the bypass approach and Dorn directs the crew. Together they confirm a safe sequence.

REN: I'll clear the quay and follow the knock.

TAMSIN: I can reach that gate.

DORN: I stop the powered stroke on your signal.

TAMSIN: Return first. Pump off. Then the restraint.

Page 8 of 12

Tamsin crosses unstable boards with the borrowed kiln staff, catches a fixed rail and pulls herself toward the bypass wheel.

SFX: CREAK

REN: Tamsin!

TAMSIN: Keep them moving!

DORN: No one touches the drive.

Page 9 of 12

Ren locates the blocked return without opening a pressurized fitting. Tamsin turns the bypass while Dorn watches the gauge fall.

REN: Here. The cap's holding pressure.

REN: Not until the gate moves.

SFX: GRRNN

DORN: Stop the stroke. Now.

Page 10 of 12

Water returns to the old channel. Dorn disconnects the drive and Bellray stops straining against its restraint.

SFX: WHUMM

TAMSIN: Slow. Let it take the flow.

DORN: Feed dark.

REN: Now we can help it.

Page 11 of 12

With power removed, Dorn releases Bellray into an open maintenance pool and signs an incident report. Tamsin studies the old tidal-exchange plate.

DORN: I thought we were protecting the quay.

REN: You still can.

DORN: Take a copy. If they ask, I gave it to you.

TAMSIN: The archive should have this.

Page 12 of 12

After the rescue, Dorn recognizes Tamsin from her earlier service. Ren turns toward her, and the trust between the pair is suddenly uncertain.

DORN: Marr. You used to escort the museum wagons.

REN: You knew these people?

TAMSIN: Yes.

REN: Before we go anywhere else, tell me.

CAPTION: NEXT: THE PRICE OF A NAME.
