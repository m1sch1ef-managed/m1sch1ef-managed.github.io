Read the text transcript

Scene summaries and dialogue, in reading order. Contains this edition’s story.

Cover

Ren Calder, Tamsin and the small copper creature Latch on the cover of The Key That Bit Back.

Page 1 of 12

At a harbor stall, Ren honestly appraises Pella’s brass valve. Tamsin arrives with a work notice.

CAPTION: SILTMARKET. WEST QUAY.

PELLA: I was told this controlled the sea.

REN: It controls a kiln vent, Pella.

SIGN: RELIC APPRAISALS

REN: Modern teeth. Old brass, not ancient magic.

PELLA: So it's worthless.

REN: Still useful. I'll fit it at your kiln.

PELLA: For what I can pay?

REN: For what the work is worth.

TAMSIN: Ren Calder. Is this how you pay the rent?

REN: Good afternoon to you too, Tamsin.

Page 2 of 12

Tamsin’s guild pass has been refused. With rent due and few coins left, she and Ren consider a recovery job.

TAMSIN: They refused my pass again.

REN: Does the harbor guild miss its best ranger?

TAMSIN: Former ranger. I gave the badge back.

REN: They've got a long memory.

TAMSIN: Three weeks' rent. The loftkeeper wants it by dawn.

REN: My last client wants a second opinion instead of paying.

TAMSIN: One recovery. Enough to clear it.

REN: What's the catch?

Page 3 of 12

The pair accept the job and enter coastal ruins. Tamsin checks the tide; Ren carries their last heatstone.

TAMSIN: One brass key. West breakwater vault. Return intact.

REN: Anonymous buyer. Generous fee.

TAMSIN: The tide gives us minutes. That's the fee.

REN: I identify it. You get us out.

TAMSIN: And you follow my lead.

REN: Last heatstone. Half a charge.

TAMSIN: Keep some for tonight.

REN: A key, rent, and a bed.

TAMSIN: Key first. Bed later.

Page 4 of 12

Inside the vault, the apparent brass key uncurls into a shivering creature chained to a stone warden.

CAPTION: LOW TIDE. FIVE MINUTES LEFT.

TAMSIN: One brass key. One month's rent.

REN: I like the sound of rent.

TAMSIN: Don't touch the black iron.

REN: Since when do keys need chains?

SFX: TIK

REN: Tamsin... keys don't shiver.

Page 5 of 12

Ren tries the clamp. The warden wakes, strikes the pedestal and triggers the closing exit.

TAMSIN: It's feeding that thing.

REN: It's barely breathing.

REN: Easy. I've got you.

SFX: KLIK

SFX: KRRROM

TAMSIN: Down!

REN: That wasn't a keyhole.

TAMSIN: I noticed.

Page 6 of 12

As Tamsin holds the warden off, Ren realizes the creature needs heat and chooses to give up their last heatstone.

TAMSIN: The door's closing!

REN: You don't want gold.

REN: You want heat.

TAMSIN: That's our last heatstone.

TAMSIN: It won't recharge.

REN: I'd rather lose the stone.

Page 7 of 12

The creature absorbs the heat, bites through its clamp and stops the warden. Tamsin pulls Ren toward the exit.

REN: Take it.

SFX: FSSSH

SFX: KRAK

TAMSIN: Now!

Page 8 of 12

Tamsin sacrifices her spear to hold the door. Both escape with the creature, which they name Latch.

SFX: GRRNNK

REN: Your spear!

TAMSIN: Keep moving.

SFX: WHOOOSH

TAMSIN: No key. No heatstone. No spear.

REN: Everyone breathing, though.

TAMSIN: What's it called?

REN: Latch.

TAMSIN: Of course it is.

Page 9 of 12

Ren apologizes for endangering Tamsin. They agree that next time they will choose together.

REN: Your wrist?

TAMSIN: Bruised. The spear took the worst.

REN: I'll buy you another.

TAMSIN: That one took six months.

TAMSIN: You touched the clamp after I warned you.

REN: I saw it suffering. I stopped looking at the room.

REN: I put you in danger. I'm sorry.

TAMSIN: Next time, we choose together.

Page 10 of 12

At the Recovery House, the clerk offers their rent in exchange for Latch. Together they cancel the job instead.

REN: Your key was alive. Someone wired it into a warden.

CLERK: The order is for an intact delivery.

TAMSIN: We nearly died recovering it.

CLERK: No delivery, no payment.

CLERK: Leave it here. You'll have your rent.

REN: And what happens to it?

CLERK: The buyer's business.

TAMSIN: Then cancel the job.

REN: Both our names.

Page 11 of 12

The pair shelter Latch beside a bathhouse pipe and plan to find Pella. Tamsin notices a mark on the broken clamp.

REN: The bathhouse vent. It's warm.

TAMSIN: Until the night valve shuts.

REN: Pella's kiln, then?

TAMSIN: She'll want more than a sad story.

REN: I still owe her a valve fitting.

REN: You don't have to stay.

TAMSIN: You owe me a spear. I'm keeping you alive to pay for it.

REN: At least we can sell the iron.

TAMSIN: Wait. Let me see that mark.

Page 12 of 12

The clamp bears the buyer’s mark and number nine. Across the harbor, Curator Vey orders the living specimen returned.

TAMSIN: The buyer's mark. On its chain.

REN: He knew it was alive.

TAMSIN: Number nine.

TAMSIN: How many others are there?

REN: First, we keep this one alive.

CAPTION: CURATOR VEY.

CURATOR VEY: Bring back the specimen, alive.

CAPTION: NEXT: THE BORROWED FLAME.
