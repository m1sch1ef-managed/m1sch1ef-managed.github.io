Read the text transcript

Scene summaries and dialogue, in reading order. Contains this edition’s story.

Cover

The Borrowed Flame, Edition 2 of The Hollow Hoard: cover.

Page 1 of 12

The bathhouse heat shuts off. Ren and Tamsin carry the cooling Latch toward the kiln district as a retrieval worker follows.

CAPTION: SILTMARKET. THE SAME NIGHT.

REN: Just a little longer.

BATHHOUSE KEEPER: Last vent. We're closed.

TAMSIN: Pella's. Now.

REN: I know.

WORKER: Found the appraiser.

Page 2 of 12

Tamsin spots their pursuer on a bridge. The pair evade the recovery order and turn into Pella’s workshop lane.

TAMSIN: Don't look back.

WORKER: Unreturned property. Hand it over.

REN: It's not property.

TAMSIN: Keep walking.

REN: She'll charge for this.

TAMSIN: Good. Then we can earn it.

Page 3 of 12

Pella opens the kiln workshop, sees the exhausted Latch and offers Ren a repair job instead of free fuel.

PELLA: You said you'd fit a valve. Not arrive with a menagerie.

REN: It needs warmth. We'll work.

PELLA: No fuel till dawn. The old spring-water return is cold too.

PELLA: Fix the return. You get food and the loft.

REN: Fair.

Page 4 of 12

Ren finds a cold return pipe capped during museum works. Upstairs, Tamsin watches the door while Latch rests beside protected warmth.

REN: Heat reaches here. It isn't reaching your bench.

PELLA: They called that a safety improvement.

REN: This cap's new. The blockage isn't age.

TAMSIN: We've got company.

Page 5 of 12

Captain Dane brings the recovery order to Pella’s door. Tamsin recognizes him and keeps the creature out of sight.

DANE: Marr. I thought you'd left the guild.

TAMSIN: I did. You still collecting for private buyers?

DANE: I collect what the order names.

PELLA: Your order names a key. My workshop isn't a crate.

Page 6 of 12

Ren and Tamsin agree on a plan: she will keep the maintenance exit clear while he repairs the heat return.

TAMSIN: I can draw them to the yard.

REN: How long do you need?

TAMSIN: Long enough to get behind them.

REN: I'll leave that unlatched. If the pipe fails, we both stop.

TAMSIN: Together, then.

Page 7 of 12

Tamsin draws Dane into the delivery yard with an empty kiln cart, then uses a borrowed staff to reach the maintenance path.

SFX: CLATTER

DANE: Close the far gate. Don't rush her.

TAMSIN: Still slow at corners, Dane.

DANE: And still too sure of yourself.

Page 8 of 12

Ren isolates the hot pipe, waits for the pressure to fall and clears the return. Pella assists with the repair.

SFX: HSSS

REN: Too much pressure.

REN: Not the fast way.

PELLA: You know this isn't a relic?

REN: It still has to work.

REN: Water first. Your vent after.

Page 9 of 12

Tamsin rings the work bell before workers reach the open hot-pipe hatch. Ren and Pella carefully test the repaired return.

DANE: Wrong direction. They're below us.

SFX: CLANG

DANE: Stay back. That's a live line.

REN: Tell me the moment it moves.

Page 10 of 12

Warm water flows again and Latch revives. Dane leaves a return notice while Pella closes the workshop door.

PELLA: Steady. No leak.

REN: There you are.

DANE: This isn't over, Marr.

TAMSIN: Then bring a better order.

PELLA: My floor. My rules. Wash before you eat.

Page 11 of 12

The three share a late meal. Ren keeps his promise to fit Pella’s valve, and she offers the loft in exchange for maintenance work.

TAMSIN: You bought us a night.

REN: We earned it.

PELLA: And that?

REN: Still included.

REN: Not much like your spear.

TAMSIN: It held.

PELLA: Work while you stay. Settle your old rent in installments.

TAMSIN: We can do that.

Page 12 of 12

Pella recognizes the mark on Latch’s clamp and shows an auction notice for specimen 002, listed beside a crown.

PELLA: I've seen that. On a consignment notice.

REN: Another key?

PELLA: A crown this time.

TAMSIN: If one object was alive...

REN: Tomorrow we go as buyers.

CAPTION: NEXT: THE COUNTERFEIT CROWN.
