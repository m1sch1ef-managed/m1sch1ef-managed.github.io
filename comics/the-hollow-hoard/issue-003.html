Read the text transcript

Scene summaries and dialogue, in reading order. Contains this edition’s story.

Cover

The Counterfeit Crown, Edition 3 of The Hollow Hoard: cover.

Page 1 of 12

An auction crown projects a royal figure. Ren joins the appraisers while Tamsin checks the loading route and Latch stays safe with Pella.

AUCTIONEER: The last king's memories. Preserved for a thousand years.

REN: A thousand years. Conveniently hard to verify.

TAMSIN: Inspection. Evidence. Out. In that order.

PELLA: They'll come back. Try not to eat my hinges.

Page 2 of 12

Ren receives a consignment manifest bearing the familiar buyer’s mark. He spots Dane above the hall and signals Tamsin near the exit.

CLERK: Initial the provenance. Keep your copy.

REN: Vey Collection.

REN: We have an audience.

TAMSIN: Then keep yours busy.

Page 3 of 12

An auction agent offers Ren a permanent appraisal desk if he certifies the crown. Ren asks to inspect it first.

AGENT: Calder. We need a reliable signature.

AGENT: A salary. Proper tools. No quay fees.

AGENT: Confirm the age. Start tomorrow.

REN: Let me see what I'm signing.

Page 4 of 12

Ren notices movement beneath the crown’s housing. Its supposedly ancient king repeats a gesture just made by the auctioneer.

REN: Something's moving.

REN: Do that again.

AUCTIONEER: Is this part of your valuation?

REN: It isn't remembering a king. It's reflecting you.

Page 5 of 12

Inside the crown, Ren finds a restrained living moth whose wings replay recent images. The agent presses him to accept the salary.

REN: There's an animal inside.

AGENT: A living optic. Historically accurate craftsmanship.

REN: You advertised memories. You knew people would hear something else.

AGENT: People are paying for a story, Calder.

Page 6 of 12

Ren refuses to sign and demonstrates the false history to the bidders. A handler moves to restrain the moth more tightly.

REN: Then they deserve the true one.

REN: Ask it to show an event from before we entered.

BIDDER: Then what am I bidding on?

AGENT: End the inspection.

Page 7 of 12

Tamsin brings a padded box. Ren disconnects the display’s power before releasing the catch and freeing the moth.

TAMSIN: Transport clearance.

REN: Pull the feed first.

SFX: CLICK

REN: Your choice. This way.

Page 8 of 12

Angry bidders confront the agent while Dane protects people in the crowd. Tamsin and Ren escape through the loading door.

BIDDER: You certified this.

DANE: Back. Give him room.

TAMSIN: Ren. Exit.

AGENT: That offer won't come again.

REN: I know.

Page 9 of 12

Outside, Ren loses his appraisal access but keeps the inspection manifest. The pair carry the rescued moth away.

TAMSIN: Any other jobs you want to decline today?

REN: They've decided for me.

DANE: Calder released the specimen. Tell Vey.

TAMSIN: You kept the records?

REN: The copy they gave me.

Page 10 of 12

At Pella’s kiln, Mirror Moth rests in a shaded nook. Its reflected workshop image fades as time passes.

PELLA: One more. Still no menagerie.

REN: It keeps what it just saw.

TAMSIN: Not ancient memories.

REN: Not even yesterday.

PELLA: Let it rest.

Page 11 of 12

Ren and Tamsin compare the manifest with Latch’s clamp. The recorded transfer happened before their brass-key recovery job was posted.

REN: Specimen nine. Coastal warden trial. Return pending.

TAMSIN: Their trial. Their chain.

REN: They left it there. Then hired us to fetch it.

TAMSIN: Vey's testing living things as parts.

Page 12 of 12

The next transport names Bell Row and specimen 003. With sundown approaching, the pair head for the pump station.

REN: Another transfer. Today.

TAMSIN: Sundown. We can still make it.

AGENT: He wouldn't certify it.

VEY: Then remove his access.

CAPTION: NEXT: THE BEAST BENEATH BELL ROW.
